About Us

Building Strong Foundations for a Better Future

Who We Are

Welcome to shiruconcretes, a trusted provider of quality concrete products and construction solutions in Kenya.

We specialize in the production and supply of durable and reliable concrete products for residential, commercial and infrastructure construction projects.

Our goal is to make quality construction materials accessible while providing excellent customer service and reliable delivery.

From individual homeowners to contractors and property developers, we work with customers of different project sizes and requirements.

What We Do

We provide quality concrete and precast products suitable for a wide range of construction projects.

Concrete Blocks

Strong and durable concrete blocks suitable for residential and commercial construction.

Paving Blocks

Quality paving blocks suitable for driveways, walkways, compounds and parking areas.

Concrete Pillars

Durable concrete pillars suitable for fencing, boundary walls and construction projects.

Precast Products

Reliable precast concrete solutions designed for different construction requirements.

Our Mission
